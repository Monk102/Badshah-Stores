# Badshah Stores - Official Showcase Website

A responsive, modern single-page showcase website for **Badshah Stores**, an established offline retail store located in **Kothi Bazaar, Betul, Madhya Pradesh, India**.

Designed to maximize local foot traffic, display product categories, and convert local inquiries into instant WhatsApp chats.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Header & Navigation**:
   - Sticky navbar with brand logo, royal crest, and quick section links.
   - Live **"Open Now / Closed Now"** dynamic status badge based on local device time.
   - Direct header WhatsApp CTA.
   - Mobile-responsive sliding navigation drawer with backdrop blur.

2. **Hero Section**:
   - Localized headline tailored to Betul: *"Badshah Stores – Your Trusted Cosmetics & Lifestyle Destination in Betul"*.
   - Subheadline referencing key landmarks: Cement Road, opposite Shyam Fashion Mall, Kothi Bazaar.
   - Dual primary CTAs: *"Get Store Directions"* (smooth scrolls to map) and *"Browse Categories"*.
   - Floating glassmorphism cards showcasing Swiss Beauty partnership and parking availability.

3. **Local Trust Highlights (3-Column Banner)**:
   - 📍 Located in Betul (Easy two-wheeler & four-wheeler parking on Cement Road).
   - 💬 Instant WhatsApp support & in-store counter pickup.
   - ⭐ Trusted local business legacy with 4.9/5 customer rating.

4. **Product & Inventory Showcase**:
   - Interactive live search bar with clear button.
   - Category filtering pills: *Cosmetics & Bridal Makeup*, *Skincare & Haircare*, *Luxury Perfumes & Attar*, *Jewellery & Hair Accessories*, *Bags & Travel Essentials*, and *Personal Care & Hosiery*.
   - Custom WhatsApp link on every card pre-populating an inquiry message (e.g., *"Namaste Badshah Stores, I'd like to check availability for Cosmetics & Bridal Makeup"*).

5. **Store Tour Visual Gallery / Carousel**:
   - Curated high-resolution imagery representing welcoming store aisles, cosmetics display, fragrance collection, and handbag racks.
   - Navigation controls: Previous / Next buttons, dynamic dot indicators, touch-swipe gestures on mobile devices, and auto-play with pause-on-hover.

6. **Location, Hours & Local SEO Section**:
   - **Real-Time Dynamic Open/Closed Status**:
     - Operating timings: **Monday – Sunday, 10:00 AM – 9:30 PM (IST)**.
     - Live indicator displays whether the store is open, remaining hours until closing, or opening time tomorrow.
   - **Full Local Address & Landmarks**: Cement Road, In Front of Shyam Fashion Mall, Kothi Bazaar, Betul, MP - 460001.
   - One-click **"Copy Address"** with toast notification feedback.
   - One-click **"Open in Google Maps"** & **"Start GPS"**.
   - Embedded interactive Google Map centered directly on Cement Road, Kothi Bazaar, Betul.
   - Transit distances: ~2.2 km from Betul Railway Station and ~1.8 km from Bus Stand.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

You can simply double-click `index.html` to open it in any web browser, or serve it using Python:

```bash
python -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit [http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000) in your browser.
